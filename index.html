Management Information Systems (MIS) is a computer-based system that moves information about people, products, and processes across the company to facilitate decision-making and problem-solving. This website will explain the importance of MIS and how technology is used in workplace and academic settings. Artificial Intelligence helps analyze data, automate repetitive tasks, and support complex problems. As AI use increases, we will explore its ethical boundaries and how to use it responsibly. We will also discuss how AI can support students and employees while recognizing the importance of originality and personalization. Overall, it will show how MIS, AI ethics, and security work together to help organizations use technology effectively.
