About Me

Nana Fujita

Through this project, I learned how Management Information Systems (MIS) streamline data management and drive informed decision-making. I also understand how professionals and students utilize artificial intelligence (AI) to boost their productivity and have support when learning difficult concepts. However, leveraging AI requires strict ethical responsibility and AI should only be used to enhance our learning, not replace our thoughts. Furthermore, I was able to explore the importance of cybersecurity and how it protects sensitive information against malware threats. Understanding essential security practices, such as maintaining strong passwords, highlighted how proactive steps protect valuable information. Overall, I feel better equipped to apply this knowledge to navigate today’s digital world ethically.
