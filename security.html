Malware and Cyber Threats

The History of Malware

Malware, short for malicious software, has posed a severe cybersecurity threat. The history of cyber threats began in the early 1970s with the Creeper program, an experimental self-replicating application that spread across networked systems. Over subsequent decades, malware evolved into different categories, such as file-damaging viruses, data-harvesting spyware, and financially crippling ransomware. Today, these programs threaten supply chains and essential facilities, demonstrating that robust cybersecurity is vital for public safety.
